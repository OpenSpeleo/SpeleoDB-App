A browser update is needed

SpeleoDB couldn't open. Your device's built-in browser may need an update.

  1. On Android, open your app store and update Android System WebView and Google Chrome. On iPhone or iPad, install the latest available system update.
  2. Close SpeleoDB completely, then open it again.

If no update is available, check for an Android system update or contact your device administrator.

You do not need to uninstall SpeleoDB or clear its data.
