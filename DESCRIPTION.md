# SpeleoDB

Take your cave surveys into the field.

SpeleoDB is the companion app for speleodb.org. It syncs your survey projects to
a satellite map on your phone and caches everything locally — so it all works
underground, off-grid, wherever you actually need it.

## What you get

- All your projects on an interactive satellite map
- Landmarks, stations, exploration leads, and cylinder installs with detail on
  tap
- Offline mode that just works — cached tiles, cached data, no drama
- Pre-download map tiles before you head out
- Settings sync action to refresh when you're back online
- Project panel to toggle what's visible on the map

## Who it's for

Cavers. Surveyors. Karst researchers. Dive teams. Anyone who publishes survey
data on SpeleoDB and wants it in their pocket when they're in the field.

## Why bother

You can't check a website from inside a cave. This app makes sure you don't have
to.
